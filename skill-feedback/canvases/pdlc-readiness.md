# Skill Canvas: pdlc-readiness

**Date:** 2026-10-05

## Who needs this?

A VPE, CTO, or head of AI adoption already using spec-driven development who wants to know whether it helps teams deliver software, achieve product outcomes, or both. They may bring one spec or a framework repository.

## What changes for them?

In a two-minute executive read, they can identify the workflow's real completion boundary, recognize strengths worth keeping, and choose one of at most three improvements with explicit business consequences and evidence.

## What assumption am I making?

Written specs and workflow instructions reveal enough of the intended operating model to identify useful gaps. They cannot prove actual team behavior or achieved outcomes. The assessment must keep those claims separate.
