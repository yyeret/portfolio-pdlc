# Our fixed-date policy and our probe policy contradict each other

**What happened.** Policy says `derisk-first` items may never skip `angle-test` and
fixed-date items never skip it either. `sales-objection-library` is fixed-date, and Dana
skipped the probe with a recorded reason ("internal-first piece, audience known"). The
reason is good. The policy says no.

**What we now believe.** The policy was written for externally-facing positioning pieces
and quietly applied to everything with a date on it. Either the policy is wrong or the skip
was — and pretending both are fine is how a definition of workflow becomes decoration.

**What the next cycle does differently.** The meta-loop puts this to the humans as a
one-line policy question rather than an agent editing `workflow.md` in passing. Until then
the skip stands, recorded and visible.
