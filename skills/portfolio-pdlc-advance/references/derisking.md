# De-risk a decision-critical assumption

Work with the initiative owner on one assumption at a time. Use the initiative's
**Derisking plan** and **Evidence log**, then prepare the usual decision brief.

1. **Name the decision.** Who owns it, and what choice is still open? Ask which
   desirability, viability, or feasibility assumption could change their call. If there
   is no testable hypothesis yet, use the Explore path to frame it first.
2. **Check what you know.** Name the scope, source and date of evidence relevant to that
   assumption. Keep opinion and missing or inaccessible evidence explicit. Ask the owner
   one question at a time for the missing or conflicting evidence that could change the
   decision. If the available evidence and cost of being wrong justify proceeding
   without another test, prepare an explicit skip recommendation.
3. **Choose the smallest test whose result could change the call.** Record the test
   owner, scope, end date, observable result and threshold before running it. Specify
   what would support proceeding, what would change direction, and how you will handle
   an inconclusive result. Target the actual uncertainty. Usage can support desirability,
   for example, while pricing behavior may be needed to judge viability. If the test
   needs new spend, customer exposure or a discovery extension, surface it to the
   human decision owner before making those commitments.
4. **Turn the result into a recommendation.** Run the test within its authorized scope,
   or have the test owner run it. Log dated observations, compare them with the agreed
   threshold and state what you learned and what remains uncertain.
   Recommend proceed, de-risk further, or stop/pause. If the result is missing or
   inconclusive at the end of the timebox, prepare the decision from what is known.
5. **Hand back the decision.** Write the brief, set `next_decision` and stop. The named
   human's dated decision belongs in the Decision log. Follow the Advance skill's
   transition rules once that decision exists.
