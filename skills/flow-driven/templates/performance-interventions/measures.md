# Measures

| ID | Signal | Source and use |
|---|---|---|
| item-age | Calendar days since step entry | Board. Pull or unblock in the weekly flow review. |
| evidence-coverage | Evidence exits with independent receipts divided by total evidence exits | Board and item logs. Investigate any missing receipt. |
| stopped-before-pilot | Triage-through-probe stops divided by all cohort items that either stopped before pilot or entered pilot. Report pre-pilot pivots separately. | Transition log. Inspect how early weak bets surface. |
| performance-kpi | Change from baseline for the affected group, in the unit and window defined on its card | Item source and readout. Decide whether the gain is adequate and durable. |
| adoption | Eligible work or users demonstrating the intended behaviour correctly and repeatedly divided by eligible work or users | Item readout. Hold the unit and window fixed and examine differences by cohort. |
| guardrail | Harm, errors, exceptions, risk, or shifted workload beyond the item's agreed threshold | Operational evidence. Contain or escalate when the threshold trips. |
| operating-cost | One-time and recurring spend, operating effort, and rework in the indicated window | Item estimates and actuals. Decide whether to sustain. |

A team should choose its KPI and thresholds before exposure. If comparison is weak, state the limits on attribution and which decision that affects. At the monthly review, assess flow, outcome decisions, and operational follow-ups.
