# Reduce incomplete service handoffs

## Context

Fictional practice item. Morgan, a service coordinator, reports that the fulfilment team sends some requests back for missing details. Dana owns the item. Sponsor and operating owner remain to be confirmed. Treat the report as an intake signal.

## Outcome hypothesis and measures

Discovery would measure the share of eligible requests returned for missing information and the time coordinators spend per request. A leading indicator could be the share of handoffs that contain the agreed details on the first attempt. An adoption measure could be repeated use of the new practice on eligible requests. Check whether the change shifts delay or work to coordinators. Set baselines and thresholds after sampling the current process.

## Intervention and evidence plan

Start by inspecting a sample of returned and accepted requests. Compare a shared handoff checklist with a change to the request form if the gap is confirmed. Neither direction has been selected.
