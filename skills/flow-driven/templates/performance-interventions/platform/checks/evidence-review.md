# Independent evidence review

Use another person, separate agent context, or reproducible check independent of the runner. Open the underlying evidence. Record the verifier, date, findings, and receipt on the item. Pending approval or missing evidence stays visible on the card.

| Step | Check |
|---|---|
| Intake | Find the source, requester, affected group, and concrete observation. |
| Triage | Check the searches or conversations behind remit, duplicates, and existing capability. Verify any early close with the named decision maker. |
| Discovery | Trace baseline and denominator to their source and sample the observed work. Check which causal claims remain assumptions. |
| Design | Check that success, harm, and adoption criteria were fixed before exposure and can be measured in the named cohort. Read the options and costs, including doing nothing. |
| Probe | Compare result and misses with the predeclared criterion. Identify what the test environment leaves uncertain. |
| Pilot | Reconcile eligible work and observed exposure to source data. Check the result against the original thresholds and include incidents, exceptions, and overrides. |
| Rollout | Check actual coverage, support ownership, monitoring, and the rollback or handover record against the signed scope. |
| Adoption | Recalculate the adoption denominator and inspect repeated correct use or exception handling across the observation window. Ask affected workers and the operating owner for evidence where logs are thin. |
| Outcome review | Reproduce the KPI and adoption comparison, flag other plausible causes, and verify the disposition and handover or closeout. |

Check the dated Decision log separately for the specific edge, decision maker, and authority. A valid negative result can support a stop or pivot. A positive result authorizes a move only under the workflow policy.
