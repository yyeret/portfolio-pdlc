## What belongs on this board (altitude)

Pick ONE altitude rule and state it:

- **Scale-up rule (default)**: score each candidate 1–3 on investment size, strategic
  opportunity/risk, and cross-product collaboration needed. 7–9 → Tier-1 (this board).
  5–6 → conversation. 3–4 → Tier-2 (awareness lane; empowered teams manage it).
- **Enterprise rule**: Tier-1 = passes the Baseline Gate (operating-plan priority,
  cross-BU coordination, material enterprise impact) AND at least 2 of 4 enterprise
  constraint tests: shared-capacity constraint, irreversible sequencing dependency,
  concentrated enterprise risk, executive tradeoff required.

Altitude sense-check each review: too low = tactical talk, leaders disengage; too high =
strategy happens in side channels; cruising = engaged steering at the right level.

## Guardrails

- **WIP**: respect the limits in `pdlc.md`; breaching one is a leadership conversation, not
  a footnote.
- **Risk balance**: target roughly <20>% of Tier-1 capacity in explore+discovery vs <80>%
  in committed build-and-deliver — manage the split intentionally, like a financial
  portfolio.
- **Outcome standard**: Tier-1 cards steer on outcome hypotheses and leading indicators.
  Activity-framed cards get flagged and rewritten, not tolerated.
- **Evidence over opinion**: confident-but-unevidenced clarity counts as a question mark.
