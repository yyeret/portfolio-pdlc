# Studio owners get admin hours back via an AI front desk

## Problem / Opportunity

Owners spend evenings on member messages and schedule shuffles; the ones who churn cite
"drowning in admin" more than price.

## Riskiest assumptions

- Owners trust an assistant with member-facing messages — **opinion → testing** (Wizard-of-Oz
  pilot, 10 studios, ends 2026-08-29).
- Resolution quality reaches 60% without human review — **opinion** (eval harness running).

## Derisking plan

Seek alpha: attack trust + quality head-on in a 5-week timebox. Kill criterion: <40%
resolution or <5 of 10 owners opting to keep it after the pilot.
