# Portfolio Charter — FlowImpact Yoga

Anchor, not plan. Section order locked; under five minutes.

## Strategy anchor

FIY grew from one scheduling product for yoga studios into a suite serving yoga and
pilates studios plus gyms with studio offerings. The company bet: own the studio's whole
operating day (scheduling, payments, retention, instructor ops) so studios grow with us
instead of stitching point tools. Tech org ~200 people; throughput has stalled while
coordination costs grow — this portfolio exists to regain traction on the few investments
that matter.

## What belongs on this board (altitude)

Scale-up rule: score 1–3 on investment size, strategic opportunity/risk, cross-product
collaboration. 7–9 → Tier-1 here. 5–6 → conversation. 3–4 → Tier-2 awareness lane,
managed by empowered product teams.

## Decision rights & cadence

| Decision | Who makes it | When |
|---|---|---|
| Invest in discovery | Jim (CPO) | async on card readiness |
| Commit to build | Mary + Jim + eng lead | monthly portfolio review |
| Pivot / de-risk further / double down / kill-pause | same trio | any review |
| Change WIP limits, workflow, topology | same trio | on an improvement card at plan-commit |
| Everything else | the teams | continuously |

## Guardrails

- WIP limits in `pdlc.md`; a breach is a leadership conversation.
- Risk balance target: ~30% of Tier-1 capacity in explore+discovery / ~70% committed —
  reviewed quarterly, managed intentionally.
- Tier-1 cards steer on outcome hypotheses and leading indicators; activity framing gets
  flagged and rewritten.
- Evidence over opinion; unevidenced confidence is a question mark.

## Not managed at this level

Team backlogs, BAU support rotations, and sub-threshold features live with the product
teams; compliance work runs in the awareness lane unless it trips the altitude rule.
